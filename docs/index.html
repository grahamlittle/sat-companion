How it coaches

It builds the skill, not the answer key.

A repeatable loop that turns wrong answers into method. You do the thinking; it makes sure you could do it again on a question you have never seen.

01

Diagnose

Finds where your points actually leak: a domain, a question type, timing, or careless slips. Not "practise more".

02

Teach the method

Gives the transferable approach for that question type, tied to the real content domain, never a one-off answer.

03

You try, it checks

You attempt and say why you chose it. It reads your reasoning and shows you the misconception, not just the letter.

04

Practise for real

Drills under the test's own rules: adaptive (Module 1 sets the ceiling), rights-only (never blank), on the clock.

The guardrails

A coach held to a line it won't cross.

The value isn't a handed answer. It's you, able to earn the point on the next question, because you own the method.

What it won't do

  • Just give you the answer letter to move on.
  • Hand you answer keys to memorise (every form is unique).
  • Promise a fixed "X correct = Y score" — scoring is adaptive.

What it will do

  • Diagnose the one or two things costing you the most points.
  • Teach the transferable method and the test-craft (Desmos, pacing, guessing).
  • Drill you the way the adaptive, rights-only test really works.

Reviewed every year

Checked against the College Board, not guessed.

The Digital SAT's structure, scoring, and tools are owned by the College Board and can change. Once a year, and any time a change is announced, the skill is checked against the official guidance at satsuite.collegeboard.org and updated. Nothing changes until it's reviewed and confirmed.
